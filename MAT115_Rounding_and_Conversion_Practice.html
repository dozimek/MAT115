Rounding Guidelines

Course protocols
  • Weight oz: nearest tenth.
  • kg: over 10 kg → nearest tenth; under 10 kg → nearest thousandth.
  • g: nearest whole gram.
  • BSA (m²): nearest hundredth.
  • mL: under 1 mL → nearest hundredth; 1 mL or more → nearest tenth.
  • gtt/min: nearest whole number.
  • mL/hr: nearest tenth.
  • mcg/kg/min: nearest tenth.
